## 🎯 The Problem

### Invisible Mental Health Crisis

In the workplace, **emotional and mental distress remains invisible**.

- Stress, mental fatigue, and burnout don't appear immediately
- Managers overestimate their ability to understand how teams truly feel
- This gap leads to:
  - Communication breakdowns
  - Poor workload distribution
  - Decreased team performance
  - Burnout and exhaustion

### The Real Issue

Stress at work is often **misread, poorly measured, and detected too late**.

**We lack simple, regular, non-intrusive tools to make weak signals visible before they become critical.**

---

## 💡 The Solution: Mindly

### Daily Mental Check-In Platform

A **lightweight, privacy-first** app designed for HR teams to monitor employee wellbeing.

### How It Works

1. **Daily Notification** - Employees receive one notification per day at their chosen time
2. **Quick Check-In** - Answer 4 simple questions in seconds:
   - Emotional state 😊😐😔
   - Energy level (1-10)
   - Stress level (1-10)
   - Mental load (1-10)
3. **Smart Analytics** - HR dashboard reveals trends, gaps, and actionable insights

### Key Features

- **Privacy-First**: Employee-controlled timing
- **Non-Intrusive**: Takes seconds, not minutes
- **Preventive**: Catch issues before they escalate
- **Actionable**: Real-time alerts for at-risk team members
